## 6 · `06-tip-not-secure.png`

Website tip #1: open your site in Chrome and look at the address bar. If it says "Not secure", many visitors will leave before reading a word.

The fix is a security certificate (HTTPS). It's free, and any decent website should include it. Every site I build has it from day one.

—
🇦🇷 Consejo #1: si Chrome dice "No seguro" al lado de tu web, mucha gente se va. Se soluciona con un certificado de seguridad (HTTPS), que es gratis.

#websitetips #smallbusinesstips #https #webdesign

## 9 · `09-tip-domain.png`

Website tip #2: check when your domain renews.

If your web address expires, your website disappears, and emails to you@yourbusiness.com can start bouncing too. While researching, we found dozens of small businesses whose domain had lapsed without them noticing.

Put the renewal date in your calendar, or ask whoever built your site.

—
🇦🇷 Consejo #2: fijate cuándo vence tu dominio. Si vence, se cae la web y también pueden dejar de llegarte los mails.

#websitetips #domain #smallbusinesstips #emailtips

## 11 · `11-tip-phone.png`

Website tip #3: open your website on your phone right now. Can you call your business with one tap?

Most customers find you on a phone, often in a hurry. Your number (or WhatsApp) should be one tap away on every page, not hidden at the bottom of a contact page.
